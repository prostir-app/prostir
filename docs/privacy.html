Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026.

In short

  • Prostir has no accounts and no servers of its own, and sends nothing about you to its author or anyone else.
  • Your headset and your PC talk to each other directly, encrypted, over your own network or a USB cable.
  • Only if you agree, the PC sends one anonymous count a day. It asks first, and sends nothing unless you answer Yes.
  • The microphone, PC notifications in the headset and sound from the screen work only when you turn them on.

Counting users

To know how many people use Prostir, the PC program sends one request a day to GoatCounter, when a headset connects. The request contains only the path /day/ followed by the date, and the very first time also /install. It carries no identifier, host name, settings or anything else about you or your PC. GoatCounter does not store IP addresses. The totals are public on this site.

It is off until you agree. The first time the settings window opens, it asks "Count this PC among Prostir's users?", says what would be sent, links to this page, and offers two equal answers, Yes and No, neither chosen in advance. Nothing is sent unless you answer Yes, so a PC where nobody answered (one that only runs Prostir in the background, a server) never counts. Change the answer at any time in the settings window (Overview, "Count this PC among users"), with the tray item of the same name, or with prostir ctl count yes or no. The environment variable PROSTIR_NO_COUNT=1 turns it off whatever the answer. Every build asks first, so a release build made from the source code may count too; debug builds never count. A distribution that does not want counting builds Prostir without its count feature, and then the question is never asked.

Version 0.1 counted until it was turned off. After an update from it, the settings window asks once as well and nothing is sent unless you answer Yes; if you had turned counting off in 0.1, it stays off and you are not asked.

The key the counts are sent with is in Prostir's source code, and so in every build. Anyone can read it, but it can only add counts, so the worst misuse is inflated numbers. Read the totals on this site as approximate.

The legal basis of the count is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), given with Yes. You can withdraw it at any time, as easily as you gave it: turn off "Count this PC among users" on the Overview page of the settings window or in the tray, or run prostir ctl count no. No count is sent after that.

What travels between the headset and your PC

The picture of your virtual monitors and detached windows, PC sound, the headset microphone (see below), which monitor has the mouse, where the mouse pointer is when you press "Mouse?", whether the headset is being worn, the headset's battery level (shown in the PC's tray), the version numbers of both apps, the settings you change in the headset menu, and short messages the PC shows in the headset, such as "Screenshot saved". All of it goes directly between your two devices, over your local network or a USB cable, encrypted with the Noise protocol. To be found by the headset, the PC broadcasts its host name on the local network.

Screenshots you take from the headset are saved on your PC, in Pictures/Prostir, and go nowhere else.

Microphone

The headset records only while an app on your PC is recording from the "Prostir headset microphone" device, only if you allowed microphone access, and only while the microphone switch in the headset menu (Quick, or Settings → Sound) is on. The headset itself enforces that switch: while it is off, the headset does not record, whatever the PC asks. The sound goes to your PC and nowhere else.

PC notifications in the headset

Off by default (Settings → PC). When you turn it on, the PC reads the notifications other apps show on your desktop and passes each one's app name, title and text to the headset, which shows it for a few seconds and keeps nothing. Turn it off, or disconnect the headset, and the PC stops reading them.

Sound from the screen

Off by default (Settings → Sound). When you turn it on, the PC checks every second and a half which programs are playing sound and on which monitor their windows are, asking PipeWire and your desktop (KDE Plasma or Hyprland). That stays on your PC: only the number of the monitor goes to the headset.

Cameras and hand tracking

The room backgrounds and the keyboard window use the Quest's passthrough. The headset system draws the camera image; Prostir never receives camera images. Hand tracking data is used on the headset to draw your hands, show the palm menu and let you point and touch. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

What is stored

On the headset, inside the app: your settings, profiles, monitor positions and the keys of paired PCs. On the PC, in ~/.config/prostir: the monitor setup, settings and the keys of paired headsets, and your screenshots in Pictures/Prostir. To delete everything, uninstall the app from the headset and delete that folder on the PC; the tray item "Forget paired headsets" removes the pairings only, and Settings → PC in the headset forgets a paired PC.

Meta Horizon Store

If you install Prostir from the Meta Horizon Store, the app asks Meta's platform whether your account owns it when it starts. That check is between the headset and Meta, under Meta's privacy policy. The app sees the answer, because that is how it knows the account owns it, but it does not store it or send it anywhere.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps server logs under GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are served from this site too. The user numbers on the home page are read by your browser from GoatCounter's public counter. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Questions and requests

Prostir and this site are maintained by the GitHub user OlexiyOdarchuk. Prostir has no servers of its own, so it holds nothing about you that could be looked up or deleted; what stays on your own devices is described above.

For a private question or request, or to report a security problem, use GitHub's private reporting: report privately. Only the maintainers see it. For anything else, open an issue at github.com/prostir-app/prostir. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
